### Midnight Massage Stories: The Weird, the Wild, and the Wonderful
As a massage therapist in Manhattan, I've seen it all - from the stressed-out executives to the exhausted athletes, and even the occasional sleep-deprived new parent. But there's something special about the midnight massage crowd. Maybe it's the fact that they're willing to venture out into the night for a little R&R, or maybe it's just that they're more likely to be wearing ridiculous pajamas. Either way, I've got some stories to tell.

#### The Great Pizza Sauce Incident
I remember one particularly memorable midnight massage session with a client who had just devoured an entire pizza by himself. I mean, we've all been there, right? The problem was, he didn't quite make it to the bathroom in time to wash his hands, and let's just say that my table got a bit of a saucy surprise. I'm pretty sure I'm still finding pizza sauce stains on my sheets to this day. But hey, at least he was relaxed, right?

#### The Snorer
Then there was the client who fell asleep the moment he hit the table. I'm talking instant snores, people. It was like he had been waiting his whole life for this exact moment to catch some Z's. The problem was, he was also one of those "chainsaw" snorers - you know, the kind that can saw down a tree in under 5 seconds. I had to stifle a giggle every time he let out a particularly loud snort, but hey, at least he was getting some rest.

#### The Karaoke King
And who could forget the client who insisted on singing along to the music I was playing in the background? I mean, I've heard some interesting renditions of "I Will Survive" in my time, but this guy took the cake. He was belting out the lyrics like he was on stage at Carnegie Hall, complete with dramatic arm flailing and off-key warbling. I was laughing so hard I had to hold onto the table for support.
